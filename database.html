Where the names come from

A 2021 crawl of 28,089 websites (the CookieBlock dataset) and our own 2026 crawls of the Tranco top 20,000. Every labeled cookie casts one vote for its name; the majority wins and ties are dropped.

Where the labels come from

The website itself. Sites running Cookiebot or OneTrust publish what each cookie is for. Analytics and Advertising count as tracker; Necessary and Functional as not a tracker. Sites sometimes disagree, so the votes are kept.

What is not here

Cookie values. The crawler held them in memory only to scan every saved file for leaks, 1,860 times, and every scan passed. Also absent: anything from a login, a real account, or a site that blocked the visit.
